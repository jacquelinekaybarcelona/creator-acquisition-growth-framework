# Creator Marketing KPI Reporting Framework

## Objective

Measure creator recruitment effectiveness, campaign performance, and contribution to SaaS customer acquisition.

This framework supports decisions about which creator segments, content formats, and acquisition channels should be improved, repeated, or discontinued.

## 1. Creator Recruitment Metrics

| KPI | Formula | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification Rate | Qualified Creators / Evaluated Creators × 100 | Measures creator sourcing quality |
| Response Rate | Creators Who Replied / Creators Contacted × 100 | Evaluates outreach effectiveness |
| Positive Response Rate | Interested Creators / Creators Contacted × 100 | Measures partnership interest |
| Onboarding Rate | Onboarded Creators / Interested Creators × 100 | Measures recruitment conversion |
| Activation Rate | Creators Publishing First Agreed Content / Onboarded Creators × 100 | Measures onboarding-to-publication effectiveness |

## 2. Campaign Performance Metrics

| KPI | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Content Publication Rate | Published Deliverables / Agreed Deliverables × 100 |
| Reach | Unique accounts exposed to campaign content, where available |
| Engagement Rate | Engagements / Impressions × 100, using consistent platform definitions |
| Referral Clicks | Tracked clicks from creator-specific links |
| Click-to-Signup Rate | Attributed Signups / Tracked Referral Clicks × 100 |

## 3. Acquisition & Business Metrics

| KPI | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Cost per Qualified Signup | Total Attributable Campaign Cost / Qualified Signups |
| Signup-to-Activation Rate | Activated Users / Attributed Signups × 100 |
| Cost per Activated User | Total Attributable Campaign Cost / Activated Users |
| Creator Acquisition Contribution | Qualified Signups Attributed to Creator Campaigns / Total Qualified Signups × 100 |

Define a qualified signup and activated user before launching experiments. Include creator compensation, production, and other directly attributable costs in campaign spend.

## 4. Attribution Approach

- Use creator-specific UTM links where practical.
- Track referral clicks, signups, and meaningful product activation events.
- Define an attribution window before reporting.
- Deduplicate conversions across creators and channels.
- Document limitations such as cross-device journeys, unattributed visits, and organic brand discovery.
- Avoid treating impressions or engagement as verified customer acquisition.

## 5. Reporting Cadence

**Weekly:** Creator pipeline, outreach activity, deliverables, and campaign issues.

**Monthly:** Recruitment conversion, campaign spend, content performance, attributed signups, and acquisition costs.

**Quarterly:** Channel comparisons, repeat partnership performance, budget allocation recommendations, and experiment learnings.

## 6. Experiment Decision Framework

**Scale:** Repeat or expand when acquisition economics are acceptable, tracking is reliable, and results show a sufficiently consistent positive signal.

**Iterate:** Adjust creator selection, creative formats, messaging, or distribution when results are promising but inconclusive.

**Pause:** Stop or redesign experiments with persistent poor performance, unacceptable costs, or major measurement limitations.

## 7. Example Reporting Scenario

A hypothetical SaaS company aims to reach 50 active creators within 12 months. The marketing team uses recruitment, content, and acquisition metrics to evaluate progress and prioritize future investment.

All targets and scenarios are illustrative. No real campaign outcomes are claimed.

## 8. Project Disclaimer

This framework is an independent, hypothetical marketing portfolio project. It does not represent actual performance data, verified attribution, or work completed for Supabase or another SaaS company.
