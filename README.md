
# Creator Acquisition & Growth Experiment Framework

A practical growth marketing case study exploring how a SaaS company could build a creator acquisition program from scratch, develop direct creator relationships, and turn successful experiments into repeatable campaigns.

**Project type:** Hypothetical marketing case study  
**Industry:** SaaS / Developer Tools  
**Target:** 50 active creators within 12 months  
**Status:** In progress

## 1. Business Challenge

A growing SaaS company wants to reduce its reliance on external influencer agencies and build direct relationships with content creators.

The challenge is to identify relevant creators, establish scalable outreach and onboarding processes, test different content formats, and measure whether creator partnerships contribute to customer acquisition.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Develop a creator sourcing and qualification framework.
- Design an outreach and onboarding workflow.
- Establish a plan to reach 50 active creators within 12 months.
- Test creator-led content formats and acquisition approaches.
- Define campaign KPIs and reporting processes.
- Identify successful experiments that could become repeatable growth programs.

## 3. Proposed Approach

### Phase 1: Research & Creator Discovery
Identify relevant creator segments, platforms, audience characteristics, and qualification criteria.

### Phase 2: Outreach & Recruitment
Develop personalized outreach, follow-up sequences, and a structured creator pipeline.

### Phase 3: Campaign Activation
Create campaign briefs, define deliverables, coordinate approvals, and establish tracking requirements.

### Phase 4: Growth Experimentation
Test content formats, messaging, creator segments, and distribution approaches.

### Phase 5: Measurement & Optimization
Evaluate creator activation, content performance, attributed acquisition, costs, and repeat participation.

## 4. Success Metrics

| Metric | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Creator response rate | Evaluate outreach effectiveness |
| Creator activation rate | Measure successful onboarding |
| Active creators | Track program scale |
| Content publication rate | Measure campaign delivery |
| Attributed signups | Assess acquisition contribution |
| Cost per acquired signup | Evaluate acquisition efficiency |
| Creator retention rate | Measure repeat participation |

## 5. Planned Project Deliverables

- Creator qualification framework
- Creator outreach and recruitment tracker
- Campaign briefing template
- Growth experiment tracker
- KPI reporting framework
