# Creator Qualification Framework

## Objective
Identify and prioritize creators who can produce credible content for a SaaS or developer tools company.

## Creator Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Maximum Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Audience relevance | 30 |
| Content quality | 20 |
| Engagement quality | 20 |
| Brand and product fit | 15 |
| Posting consistency | 10 |
| Collaboration potential | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Scoring Method
Assign points to each criterion based on documented evidence from the creator's public content, audience, and previous partnerships. Record the reasoning behind each score. If evidence is unavailable, mark it as unverified rather than assuming a positive result.

## Priority Tiers
- **80–100:** High priority — suitable for initial outreach.
- **60–79:** Review further — potential fit requiring additional evaluation.
- **0–59:** Low priority — not prioritized for the current campaign.

These thresholds are starting assumptions and should be adjusted after reviewing campaign outcomes.

## Initial Creator Segments
1. Developer educators and coding tutorial creators.
2. Indie hackers and SaaS founders sharing product-building journeys.
3. AI application builders and technical content creators.
4. Technology educators explaining developer tools to beginners.

## Qualification Workflow
1. Discover creators on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.
2. Review recent content and audience engagement.
3. Check audience relevance and potential brand conflicts.
4. Score each creator using the weighted criteria.
5. Document evidence, uncertainties, and outreach priority.
6. Shortlist high-priority creators for personalized outreach.

## Important Considerations
- Follower count alone should not determine creator quality.
- Evaluate genuine engagement rather than likes alone.
- Confirm partnership terms, disclosure requirements, and content usage rights before activation.
- Do not collect or publish private creator contact details.

## Project Note
This framework is an illustrative portfolio exercise, not a validated predictive model. No actual creator recruitment or campaign results are claimed.
