# Creator Recruitment Workflow

## Objective
Build a repeatable process for recruiting, onboarding, and activating relevant SaaS and developer-tool creators.

## Pipeline Stages

1. **Identified:** Creator discovered through platform research, referrals, or community discovery.
2. **Qualified:** Creator evaluated using the qualification scorecard and documented evidence.
3. **Contacted:** Personalized outreach sent and follow-up date recorded.
4. **Negotiation:** Creator expresses interest; deliverables, compensation, usage rights, and timelines are discussed.
5. **Onboarded:** Partnership terms confirmed and campaign brief shared.
6. **Activated:** Creator publishes their first agreed campaign content.

Track Declined, Unresponsive, and Disqualified as separate outcomes. Creators can also be paused or reactivated.

## Follow-Up Rules

- Send a first follow-up after 5 business days without a response.
- Send a final follow-up after another 5–7 business days if appropriate.
- Stop outreach when a creator declines or opts out.
- Respond to positive replies within 1–2 business days where possible.
- Record the next action, owner, and due date for each active partnership.

## Measurement Definitions

- **Qualification rate:** Qualified creators / creators evaluated × 100.
- **Response rate:** Creators who reply / creators contacted × 100.
- **Positive response rate:** Creators expressing interest / creators contacted × 100.
- **Onboarding rate:** Creators onboarded / creators expressing interest × 100.
- **Activation rate:** Creators publishing first agreed content / creators onboarded × 100.
- **Active creator count:** Unique creators who publish qualifying content within the defined reporting period.

Report these metrics by platform, creator segment, and campaign period. Deduplicate creators across campaigns and define the reporting window consistently.

## Operational Safeguards

- Confirm partnership terms and compensation before content production.
- Document advertising disclosure requirements and content usage rights.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy requirements.
- Avoid publishing personal contact information or confidential commercial terms.
- Review tracking quality before making acquisition or ROI claims.

## Example Scenario

A hypothetical SaaS company aims to establish 50 active creator partnerships within 12 months. Pipeline volumes, conversion assumptions, and outreach timing should be validated through initial experiments and adjusted using observed performance.

## Disclaimer

This is a hypothetical portfolio workflow, not evidence of a live creator acquisition program.
